# Equipment positioning and evidence

Reviewed 16 September 2026. Editorial basis for the redesign: sell aquatic exercise equipment and explain model selection, rather than imply pool construction or customization services.

## Product evidence

- [One Plus](https://poolbiking.com/en/products/poolbiking-one-plus.html): manufacturer specifications support the adjustment, progressive resistance and construction statements on the homepage. Manufacturer descriptions are not independent reliability tests.
- [Evolution](https://poolbiking.com/en/products/poolbiking-evolution.html): reinforced construction is a product-specific feature. Do not apply it to every bike or accessory.
- [Medical treadmill](https://poolbiking.com/en/products/pooltrekking-medical.html): supports and armrests are features for assessment, not a guarantee of safety or clinical suitability. Its page displays a 4-year badge but states 2 years for steel elements. The homepage also lists a 4-year badge. Removed the unqualified numeric warranty from our Medical listing pending written clarification; retain the product and explain the uncertainty.
- [Manufacturer FAQ](https://www.poolbiking.com/index_en.html): provides care guidance and warranty variation. No independently verified service life, failure rate, Caribbean spare-parts stock or service turnaround was established. Do not promise lifetime durability, maintenance-free use or immediate local support.

## Clinical evidence and its limits

- [Rewald et al., 2020 randomized trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32169459/): 111 participants aged 50–70 with mild-to-moderate knee osteoarthritis; 12-week aquatic cycling program. Reported pain and physical-function improvements versus usual care. Evidence for that intervention/population, not brand superiority or effectiveness in frail care-home residents.
- [Older-adult knee osteoarthritis trial, 2022](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9467329/): 32 men aged at least 60, eight weeks of aquatic exercise. Limited population and short follow-up constrain generalization. Do not turn balance/gait findings into claims that the equipment prevents falls.
- [South Tees NHS aquatic physiotherapy](https://www.southtees.nhs.uk/services/physiotherapy/community-outpatient-physiotherapy-middlesbrough-redcar-and-cleveland/what-treatments-are-available/what-is-hydrotherapy-aquatic-therapy/): explains buoyancy, reduced joint loading and supervised therapeutic movement. These principles do not make every commercial aquatic session physiotherapy.

## Publishing rules

Keep research links alongside the relevant website claims. Explain benefits in terms of possible participation and supported exercise. Staff should review access, transfers, fit, individual health and supervision. Never promise restored mobility, prevention of falls, pain-free exercise, universally safe use or guaranteed rehabilitation outcomes. Keep manufacturer hotel references separate from this Caribbean business’s customers.
